Yizhou Chen

Ph.D. Candidate in Robotics · University of Michigan

I am a member of the ROAHM Lab, advised by Prof. Ram Vasudevan. I work on the perception, modeling, and planning of deformable objects. My dream demo is a fully autonomous wire harness assembly.

Previously, I worked in the MMint Lab with Prof. Nima Fazeli on visuo-tactile representation learning for robotic manipulation.

TrackDeform3D: Markerless and Autonomous 3D Keypoint Tracking and Dataset Collection for Deformable Objects

Yeheng Zong, Yizhou Chen, Alexander Bowler, Chia-Tung Yang, Ram Vasudevan

IROS 2026 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems

arXiv Project page Code Video

Recovers 3D keypoints and their topology from RGB-D recordings of cables, branched cables, fabric and cloth, and tracks them without markers or object-specific training.
